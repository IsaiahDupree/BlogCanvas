## Document Structure

Each PRD follows this structure:
1. **Executive Summary** - Overview and goals
2. **Problem Statement** - What we're solving
3. **User Stories** - Detailed requirements
4. **Technical Requirements** - Database, APIs, etc.
5. **UI/UX Requirements** - Wireframes and flows
6. **Implementation Plan** - Phased approach
7. **Testing Requirements** - Quality criteria
8. **Risks & Mitigations** - Risk assessment
