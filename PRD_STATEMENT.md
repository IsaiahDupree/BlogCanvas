# BlogCanvas PRD Statement

## Original Vision Statement

> A client-vendor relationship project management suite for bloggers where:
> 
> - **Clients** can see full transparency of work vendors declare to provide
> - **Vendor-client interface** with file transfer capabilities
> - **Client research** and **client website research** functionality
> - **Thorough blog generation** with various different inputs from:
>   - Research and data
>   - SEO analysis and optimization
>   - Images pulled from different sources
>   - Templates and topic coverage assessments
>   - AI reviewing with automated and manual actions
> - **Project status** catered to vendors and clients with role-specific views
> - **Newsletters** tailorable by vendors to be sent to clients about future and/or current work
> - **Stripe integrations** for billing and payments
> - **Resend email integrations** for transactional emails
> - **Gmail integrations** for communication
> - **Vendor dev access** for technical operations

---

*This statement serves as the foundational vision for the complete BlogCanvas PRD. See `PRD_COMPLETE.md` for the full implementation requirements.*
